## Governance, Risk, and Compliance in AI Deployment

As organizations scale their reliance on **ai technology**, governance frameworks must evolve concurrently. Unchecked model hallucination, data privacy leakage, and algorithmic bias present severe legal and reputational risks.

Enterprise architects must implement stringent guardrails, including:
1. **Data Sanitization:** Stripping Personally Identifiable Information (PII) before data enters training or inference pipelines.
2. **Model Auditing:** Regularly evaluating model outputs against ethical guidelines and industry compliance standards.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Triggers:** Establishing hard stops where high-stakes financial or legal decisions require manual sign-off.

Failing to establish these guardrails can lead to severe operational vulnerabilities, making security and compliance foundational pillars of any serious AI initiative.
