## Negotiation Under Pressure: The Donovan Method

One of the most compelling aspects highlighted in **bridge of spies spielberg** is the art of high-stakes negotiation. Donovan is not a trained diplomat or a covert operative; he is a negotiator who understands leverage, human psychology, and the value of standing firm when the opposing side attempts to bully him.

When negotiating software contracts, client deliverables, or venture capital funding, professionals often cave under artificial deadlines. Donovan teaches us that true leverage comes from preparation and moral clarity. He realizes early on that the captured American pilot Francis Gary Powers and the economics student Frederic Pryor are dual assets that must be secured together. He refuses a flawed compromise, setting a brilliant precedent for corporate deal-making.
