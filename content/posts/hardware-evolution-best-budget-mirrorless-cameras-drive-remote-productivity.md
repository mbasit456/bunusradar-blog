## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why choose a mirrorless camera over a standard webcam for remote work?
Mirrorless cameras feature significantly larger physical sensors and interchangeable lenses that deliver superior low-light performance, natural background blur, and unmatched image clarity compared to standard webcams.

### Can entry-level mirrorless cameras be powered continuously for long meetings?
Yes, almost all modern mirrorless systems support continuous power via USB-C Power Delivery or dummy battery adapters that plug directly into AC wall outlets.

### Do I need expensive lenses to see a productivity improvement?
No, standard kit lenses or affordable prime lenses provide more than enough optical performance to drastically elevate your video fidelity over built-in computer cameras.

### Are these camera systems difficult to set up for daily remote calls?
Most modern devices offer plug-and-play USB streaming modes or simple clean HDMI outputs, allowing them to integrate into your existing video conferencing software with minimal configuration.
