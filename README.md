## Editorial Coverage Categories

BunusRadar organizes rigorous technical reporting across five primary verticals:

1. **[Artificial Intelligence](https://bunusradar.site/category/ai):** Deep-dive benchmark analyses comparing foundational LLMs, autonomous coding agents, open weights architectures, and generative synthetic media.
2. **[Technology & Architecture](https://bunusradar.site/category/technology):** Semiconductor developments, server hardware, edge compute distribution, and Next.js full-stack optimization.
3. **[Business & Growth](https://bunusradar.site/category/business):** Micro-SaaS unit economics, digital revenue systems, fintech infrastructure, and remote enterprise models.
4. **[Productivity & Cognitive Workflows](https://bunusradar.site/category/productivity):** High-output deep work frameworks, knowledge graph management, and systems thinking.
5. **[Lifestyle & Future of Living](https://bunusradar.site/category/lifestyle):** Digital nomad policy, ambient workspace computing, and high-performance lifestyle technology.

## Open Research & Citation Guidelines

All quantitative benchmarks, comparative charts, and empirical findings published on BunusRadar are open for academic and journalistic citation under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** license.

When referencing BunusRadar research in third-party publications or academic papers, please attribute using the canonical article URL:

```bibtex
@misc{bunusradar2026,
  author = {BunusRadar Editorial Staff},
  title = {Technology Journalism & Empirical AI Evaluation},
  year = {2026},
  publisher = {BunusRadar},
  howpublished = {\url{https://bunusradar.site}}
}
```

## Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Framework:** Next.js 14 App Router
- **Rendering:** Static Site Generation (SSG) with incremental Edge revalidation
- **Typography & Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Data Ingestion:** Markdown frontmatter pipeline via `gray-matter` and `marked`
- **Syndication:** RSS 2.0 (`/rss.xml`), Atom, and XML Sitemap protocol (`/sitemap.xml`)
